ViRAE tool

ViRAE (Viral Reads Assembly Enhancer) detects artificial rRNA–virus chimeric reads, trims the non-viral segment and keeps the viral sequence, so de novo assembly yields longer, more complete viral genomes. It evolves the Zero-Waste Algorithm (ZWA).
